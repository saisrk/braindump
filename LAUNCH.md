## 1. Product Hunt

**Tagline:** Capture what you read. Actually remember it.

**First comment (maker's note):**

Hi Product Hunt 👋

Most read-later apps are graveyards. You save a hundred articles, feel productive,
and forget every one of them by Friday.

Braindump is built to close the loop between *consuming* and *knowing*:

📥 **Capture** — paste a URL or drop in a note. AI extracts the key ideas and
files them on a shelf by topic. No tagging. No folders. It just lands where it
belongs — inside a personal library that looks and feels like books on a shelf.

🔁 **Review** — spaced repetition resurfaces each idea right before you'd forget
it. Five minutes a day keeps a library of 200+ ideas alive.

🧠 **Teach Back** — explain a concept in your own words. The AI scores your
understanding, tells you what you nailed, and surfaces exactly what you missed.
This is the wall people actually pay to move past.

⚡ **Quiz** — auto-generated multiple-choice and short-answer questions from your
own notes. Missed questions feed straight into your review queue. Pro feature.

✨ **Express** — pick a format (talking points, STAR interview story, LinkedIn bio,
or team summary) and Braindump turns your proven learnings into polished output
in seconds.

**Free to start:** 1 capture a day, up to 30 learnings, 3 teach-backs a week,
and one Express trial. Go Pro for 10 captures/day, infinite library, quizzes,
and unlimited everything else.

Would love your honest feedback — what would make you come back on day 7?

## 3. LinkedIn

**The difference between reading a lot and knowing a lot is a system.**

I read constantly. For years I had almost nothing to show for it — a graveyard of
saved links and highlights I never opened again.

The problem was never capture. Capture is easy. The problem was everything after it.

So we built **Braindump** around the full loop:

• **Capture** — paste a URL or a note; AI extracts the key ideas and shelves them
  by topic. Your library looks like actual bookshelves, not another list.
• **Review** — spaced repetition brings each idea back right before you'd forget it.
• **Teach Back** — explain a concept in your own words and get scored on exactly
  what you understand and what you're missing.
• **Quiz** — auto-generated questions from your own notes; wrong answers go straight
  into your review queue. (Pro)
• **Express** — turn proven knowledge into talking points, interview stories, or a
  profile summary on demand.

It's designed to feel like a personal library you own — not another inbox to
declare bankruptcy on.

Free to start: 1 capture a day, up to 30 learnings (lifetime), 3 teach-backs a
week, and one Express trial. Pro removes the limits and unlocks quizzes when
you're ready.

If you've ever finished a great book and realised a month later you couldn't explain
a single idea from it — this is for you.
