## 1. Product Hunt

**Tagline:** Capture what you read. Actually remember it.

**First comment (maker's note):**

Hi Product Hunt 👋

Most read-later apps are graveyards. You save a hundred articles, feel productive,
and forget every one of them by Friday.

Braindump is built to close the loop between *consuming* and *knowing*:

📥 **Capture** — paste a link or a note. AI pulls out the key ideas and shelves

understanding, then tells you exactly what you missed.

stories, or a LinkedIn bio in seconds.

the works — not another inbox.

**Free to start:** 1 capture a day, up to 30 learnings, 3 teach-backs a week,
